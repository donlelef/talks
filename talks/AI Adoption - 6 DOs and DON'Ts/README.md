# AI Adoption: 6 DOs and DON'Ts

Rolling out AI inside an organisation rarely fails for lack of technology — it fails because of how the rollout is run. This talk distills what actually moves the needle when introducing AI tools and workflows to non-technical teams, based on hands-on adoption programmes.

Attendees leave with six concrete DOs and DON'Ts they can apply immediately to their own AI adoption effort, cutting through the trial and error.
