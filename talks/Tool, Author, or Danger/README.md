# Tool, Author, or Danger? The Role of Generative AI in Academic Research

Generative AI's role in academic writing is contested territory: tool, co-author, or threat to research integrity, depending on who's asked. This talk analyzes GenAI's actual role in scholarly work, referencing Nature's 2023 authorship guidelines and the debate they triggered.

Attendees leave with a grounded position on where GenAI fits in academic research workflows — useful whether they're researchers, reviewers, or just trying to keep up with the policy debate.
