# Talking AI with a CEO

Most AI strategy content comes from analysts or academics who've never had to make an AI bet with real payroll on the line. This conversation-style talk covers AI strategy from the perspective of a founder and CEO who has: what to invest in, what to ignore, and how AI actually changes a company's roadmap.

Attendees leave with a founder's-eye view of AI strategy they can compare against their own organisation's plans.

## Where it was held

- Profession AI, Italy, 2024
- Wrocław University of Science and Technology — invited lecture, Wrocław, Poland, 2024
