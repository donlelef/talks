# Should You Trust Your Copilot? Limitations and Merits of AI Coding Assistants

AI coding assistants promise productivity gains, but they also come with licensing concerns, security-related bugs, and real questions about how they affect junior developers' learning. This talk reviews the evidence on all four fronts, separating hype from measured impact.

It's not a for-or-against talk — it's a framework for deciding when and how to trust a coding assistant, and when to double-check it harder than you'd double-check a human PR.

Attendees leave with a practical checklist for adopting AI coding assistants on their team without inheriting their worst failure modes.
