# Product Management for the GenAI Era

GenAI breaks a lot of assumptions product managers rely on: outputs aren't deterministic, quality is probabilistic, and "done" is a moving target. This talk covers how product management practice needs to change when GenAI is a core part of the product, drawing on real work done with Eni.

Attendees leave with a concrete list of product management practices to adjust — from roadmapping to QA — when GenAI stops being a feature and becomes the product.
