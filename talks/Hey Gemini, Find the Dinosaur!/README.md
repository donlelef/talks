# Hey Gemini, Find the Dinosaur!

Multimodal AI is easiest to understand through a demo that's actually fun to watch. This playful, demo-driven talk uses Google Gemini's multimodal capabilities to solve a genuinely silly task — finding a dinosaur — as a way into what multimodal models can and can't do.

Attendees leave with a hands-on, memorable feel for multimodal AI's real capabilities, well past what the marketing slides usually show.

## Where it was held

- Codemotion, Milan, Italy, 2024
