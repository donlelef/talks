# Ordo Ab Chao: The Magic of DALL-E and Midjourney

DALL-E and Midjourney feel like magic until you understand diffusion models — then they just feel like well-engineered math. This talk explains how diffusion models work, what they're genuinely good at, and where they reliably fall short, using DALL-E and Midjourney as running examples.

Attendees leave with a working mental model of diffusion models that demystifies both the impressive results and the weird failure cases.
