# AI & Psychology: Can They Nudge Towards Wellbeing?

Discusses psychological and AI applications in nutrition, medicine, and financial services, and how the two fields can nudge people towards healthier choices.

## Where it was held

- Milan Digital Week, Milan, Italy, March 2021
- Applied Machine Learning Days (AMLD), February 2021
- Seminario ISP, Banca Intesa Innovazione, 2021
