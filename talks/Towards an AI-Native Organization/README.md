# Towards an AI-Native Organization

For 150 years, income per person in the US has grown by just under 2% a year, through electricity, computers and the internet. AI is the first technology in a long time that could bend that line, and in some fields it already beats the best human experts. Yet the evidence keeps pointing to the same bottleneck: not the models, but how people and organisations work around them.

This talk walks through three shifts, each starting from data and ending on an open question. First, the relationship between people and AI: a "jagged frontier" that keeps moving, and what it means to support professionals when a machine does part of their job better. Second, the shape of organisations: companies reaching $100M in revenue with a fraction of the people they once needed, small autonomous teams and entrepreneurial generalists pulling ahead, and the much harder problem of making a whole organisation AI-native rather than a single team. Third, the dark side: AI fatigue, more focus on hard problems and more isolation, and what we are experimenting with at TeamSystem to keep the creative part of the work human and shared.
