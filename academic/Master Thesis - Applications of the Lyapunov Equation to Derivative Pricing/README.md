# Applications of the Lyapunov Equation to Derivative Pricing

A study, carried out in collaboration with A2A, on applying stochastic linear system theory to the pricing of energy derivatives. The thesis models energy commodities as stochastic linear systems and uses the Lyapunov equation to derive a closed-form solution for volatility, plugging the result into the Black-Scholes formula. The approach is then extended to portfolios of multiple assets, including spread options and Value at Risk estimation.

## Where it was published

- Master's Degree in Computer Engineering, University of Pavia, Italy, 2017
