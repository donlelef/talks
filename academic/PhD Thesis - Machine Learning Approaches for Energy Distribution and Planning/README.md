# Machine Learning Approaches for Energy Distribution and Planning

A dissertation applying machine learning to two problems in the energy transition. In collaboration with A2A, it studies the prediction of natural gas demand at country level, comparing base regression models and introducing ensembling for more accurate forecasts. In collaboration with the École Polytechnique Fédérale de Lausanne, it tackles the identification of the topology and electrical parameters of distribution power networks.

## Where it was published

- PhD in Electrical, Computer and Biomedical Engineering, University of Pavia, Italy, 2020
